## What the winners share

1. Kept climbing after week 1; peak came late (day 9-75). Faders peaked by day 5-14 and lost 73-87%.
2. They are platforms others use and pay fees into (hook platform, arbitrage hook, launchpad, lending pool, AI payments). The faders are memes or single ideas.
3. Speed of start does not tell you anything: HOOKR/WTH/FOBO jumped on day 1, PRIORS/RAETO did not.
4. A GitHub does not predict anything: only PRIORS and FOBO have real code. WTH has a README, HOOKR has docs only, RAETO a small plugin.

HOOKR and WTH launched in early August when the chain was new and everything was cheap.
