## TL;DR on every card (asked 5 Nov 2026)
Every token the scanner lists should open with a short, easy-to-read review before the score details:

- **TL;DR**: one or two plain sentences: what it is, and the verdict (worth a look / careful / skip) and why.
- **Quick info**: market cap, liquidity, 24h change, launched when and where, X age and followers,
  GitHub yes/no, token address posted by the team yes/no.
- **Watch out for**: the top 1–3 risks in plain words (new X, unaudited, thin liquidity, dev never mentions token…).

Why: in this space people decide in seconds. The dot-point reasons are good evidence but slow to read.
Model: the hand reviews done in chat on 5 Nov (MERY, OSTRA, UPOCH, SIC, FOBO).
